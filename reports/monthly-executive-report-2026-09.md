## How the headline numbers are calculated

- **Total Realized Cost Savings** = `Σ Finance-approved actual cost reductions in the period`  
  Σ of 9 Finance-approved ledger entries (pending AED 86,559 excluded) = **AED 1,005,136**
- **Confirmed Financial Loss Prevented** = `Σ verified prevented loss per incident = gross control claims − duplicate claims`  
  AED 35,918,223 − AED 25,315,366 = **AED 10,602,857**
- **Agentic Efficiency Gain** = `(Manual baseline duration − Agent end-to-end duration) ÷ Manual baseline duration × 100`  
  (4,512,833 min − 1,523,095 min) ÷ 4,512,833 min × 100 = **66.2%**
- **Human Hours Saved** = `(Σ comparable manual task minutes − Σ agent-assisted end-to-end task minutes) ÷ 60`  
  (4,512,833 min − 1,523,095 min) ÷ 60 = **49,829 h**
- **Total AI Operating Spend** = `Inference spend + Infrastructure spend + AI platform licences`  
  AED 215,022 + AED 555,155 + AED 126,957 = **AED 897,134**
- **Model Availability Percentage** = `Successful eligible inference requests ÷ Total eligible inference requests × 100`  
  (4,763,920 − 26,239 − 3,761) ÷ (4,763,920 − 26,239) × 100 = **99.921%**
